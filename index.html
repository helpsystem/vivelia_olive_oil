Customer Inquiries / Preguntas Frecuentes

Frequently Asked Questions

Natural extra virgin olive oil is rich in living chlorophyll. When exposed to ambient kitchen or supermarket illumination, photo-oxidation degrades Vitamin E and bioactive polyphenols within weeks. Our food-grade opaque tin offers 100% light-blocking barrier protection, maintaining fresh-from-the-mill terroir flavor for up to 36 months.
On the upper lid of the canister, two small plastic pull-rings lie flush. Simply pull both rings gently upward to extend the 3-centimeter silicone pouring conduit, then unscrew the hermetic seal cap. This design prevents side drips and allows seamless refilling of tabletop cruets.
Store in a cool pantry or cabinet between 57°F and 70°F (14°C - 21°C), away from direct stove heat. Do not refrigerate, as cooling temperatures cause natural waxes to precipitate and turn cloudy (though it returns to liquid gold at room temperature without compromising quality).
Every tin is encased in customized high-impact multi-chamber air column cushions and packed into reinforced five-ply export cartons. We provide a 100% No-Risk Guarantee: in the rare event of transit damage, an immediate replacement or full refund is dispatched within 24 hours.
