## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory heritage and refined elegance of ancient Mediterranean terroir tailored for modern luxury commerce. The aesthetic harmonizes the raw, organic legacy of generational Greek olive groves with the precision and restraint of haute couture editorial commerce.

The target audience comprises discerning culinary epicureans, luxury gift buyers, and health-conscious connoisseurs who demand authenticity, provenance, and uncompromising craftsmanship.

### Design Movement & Ethos
The interface bridges **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Mediterranean Classicality**. Rather than austere clinical minimalism, surfaces lean into natural sunlit warmth, generous breathing room, deliberate typographic tension between poetic high-contrast serifs and architectural grotesque sans-serifs, and delicate antique golden accents that evoke cold-pressed harvest oil glistening under Aegean light.

## Colors

The color palette captures cold-pressed olives, sun-drenched limestone, and antique Hellenic gilding.

### Primary & Secondary Surfaces
- **Arboreal Green (Primary - `#1F3025`)**: The core brand color used for primary CTA fills, header backgrounds in immersive moments, bold typographic anchors, and primary branding emblems.
- **Olive Canopy (Tertiary - `#2C4233`)**: Used for hover states, card backdrops in dark sections, subtle container accents, and interactive surfaces.
- **Antique Hellenic Gold (Secondary - `#C5A059` / Light `#D4AF37`)**: The metallic warmth used for prestigious award badges, harvest year stamps, focus rings, fine separator lines, and interactive gold foil accents.

### Neutral Foundation & Typography
- **Aegean Sand (Base Light Neutral - `#FDFBF7`)**: The default canvas background, providing an organic, unbleached parchment warmth that eliminates stark display glare.
- **Limestone Cream (Secondary Light Neutral - `#F5F0EB`)**: Used for card containers, drawer sheets, table cell alternations, and nested product details.
- **Charcoal Basalt (`#161C18`)**: High-legibility text tone delivering contrast against both sand and cream surfaces without the severity of pure `#000000`.
- **Muted Olive Neutral (`#647067`)**: Used for secondary meta-labels, volume indicators, and secondary product specifications.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between editorial prestige and functional e-commerce clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical European prestige, heritage authenticity, and bespoke craftsmanship. Headlines are italicized for sensory descriptors (e.g., *Early Harvest, Cold Pressed*).
- **Body, Commerce Meta & Pricing (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and legibility for product descriptions, nutritional data, extraction acidity percentages, checkout pathways, and cart drawers.
- **Uppercase Eyebrows (`label-caps`)**: Always rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.12em`) to ground product cards and hero banners with a luxury atelier feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical rhythm, emulating high-end print lookbooks.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and dynamic outer margins constrained to a maximum content width of `84rem` (`1344px`). Hero showcases and visual catalog spreads use generous asymmetric whitespace (e.g., `space-3xl` to `space-4xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2 columns with prominent bottle imagery and sticky quick-add triggers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and margins. Product showcases transition into smooth horizontal snapping carousels or single-column luxury viewports with full-bleed imagery and anchored bottom transaction bars.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using **tonal layered planes**, **ambient sunlit warm shadows**, and **fine gold and olive hairline borders**.

### Depth Layers
- **Surface 0 (Base Foundation)**: `#FDFBF7` (Aegean Sand canvas).
- **Surface 1 (Contained Elements & Product Cards)**: `#F5F0EB` (Limestone Cream) bordered by `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`.
- **Surface 2 (Floating Commerce Menus & Drawers)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7` with an ambient olive shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(31, 48, 37, 0.08)`.
- **Surface 3 (Modal Dialogs & Tasting Notes Overlays)**: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(22, 28, 24, 0.16)`.

### Border Rationale
Borders mimic embossed hot-stamping on specialty paper:
- Soft golden hairline: `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`.
- Deep olive container border: `1px solid rgba(31, 48, 37, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft architectural geometry** (`roundedness: 1`), providing crisp corners tempered by subtle softening. 

- **Containers, Product Cards & Input Fields**: Standardized to `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii, providing a structured, architectural foundation.
- **Pill Badges & Terroir Harvest Tags**: Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for organic origin badges, acidity metrics, harvest years, and award indicators (e.g., *Koroneiki Variety • 0.2% Acidity*).
- **Interactive Action Buttons**: Tailored with `0.25rem` micro-radii to preserve the formal elegance of luxury apothecary and olive oil vessels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Add to Collection" / "Purchase")**: Deep Arboreal Green (`#1F3025`) solid fill, cream typography (`#FDFBF7`), `0.25rem` radius, uppercase tracked font (`label-caps`). On hover: background shifts to `#2C4233` with a subtle golden glow border (`0 0 0 1px #C5A059`).
- **Secondary (Tasting Notes / Terroir Exploration)**: Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A059` border, charcoal (`#161C18`) or olive text. On hover: fills with light gold tint (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Tertiary / Link**: Baseline-aligned serif or sans text paired with a static antique gold underline (`1px`) offset by `4px`.

### Badges & Origin Pills
- **Harvest & Variety Chips**: Soft pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`) with `#F5F0EB` fill, delicate gold hairline boundary, `#1F3025` text, and an optional 6px circular dot indicator in Antique Gold.
- **Accreditation Badges (PDO / Organic / Gold Medal)**: Metallic antique gold border with deep green typography and small iconography (e.g., pressed olive branch or amphora motif).

### Product Cards
- Contained within `#F5F0EB` limestone backgrounds with `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`.
- Features an ultra-clean vertical layout: full-length bottle cut-out photography over an unblemished warm vignette backdrop.
- Upper-left contains the origin pill (e.g., *Kalamata PDO*); upper-right houses a subtle bookmark/wishlist toggle.
- Bottom section displays: Product Title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, polyphenol/acidity micro-specs in `label-caps`, volume (e.g., *500 ml / 16.9 fl oz*), followed by a flush-bottom high-conversion quick-add interface.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7` with bottom-border-only or full `1px` subtle olive border (`rgba(31, 48, 37, 0.2)`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C5A059` with a subtle golden ring blur (`0 0 0 1px #C5A059`).
- Placeholder: `#647067` in `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and refined square checkboxes with an antique gold border.
- Checked state: `#1F3025` solid fill holding an Aegean Sand checkmark or centered golden seed pip.

### Specialized Commerce Components
- **Acidity & Polyphenol Gauge**: A luxury metadata component showing polyphenol counts (e.g., *620 mg/kg*) with a fine line scale rendered in warm antique gold.
- **Harvest Batch Drawer**: An interactive slide-out panel outlining single-estate coordinates, bottle harvest dates, master presser signatures, and pairing notes.
