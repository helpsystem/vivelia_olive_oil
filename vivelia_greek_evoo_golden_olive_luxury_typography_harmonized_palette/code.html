Science & Purity Standards

Why Tin Canisters & What Sets Vivelia Apart?

The physics of freshness: Combining Aegean microclimates with pharmaceutical-grade dark storage packaging to retain maximum bio-potency.

wb_sunny

Absolute Superiority of Metal Tins over Clear Glass

Light and ultraviolet radiation are the catastrophic catalysts of photo-oxidation in extra virgin olive oil, degrading living chlorophyll, polyphenols, and Vitamin E within weeks. Vivelia’s food-grade opaque tin blocks 100% of photon penetration, ensuring the freshly pressed harvest aromas endure to the final drop.

nest_eco_leaf

The Dual Cultivar Synergy: Koroneiki & Megara

Koroneiki (the undisputed Queen of Mediterranean Olives) provides extreme polyphenol concentrations and vibrant green grassy notes. Megara olives introduce an elegant, silky fruity harmony that softens excessive astringency, yielding a well-rounded, balanced gastronomic profile.

biotech

Ultra-Low Free Acidity (< 0.28%) & Peroxide Index

While the International Olive Council (IOC) threshold for extra virgin grade is <0.8% acidity, Vivelia tests consistently between 0.25% and 0.38%. This extraordinary metric confirms bruised-free gentle hand-picking and pressing under 26°C within 4 hours.
